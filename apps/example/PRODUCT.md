# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers building Void apps who are deciding whether to add void-fbtee. The events app is a
demo of void-fbtee, not a product in its own right.

## Product Purpose

Show that void-fbtee gives a Void app real i18n, and let developers see it working before they
add it to their own app.

## Positioning

Real i18n that works the Void way, in one package: strings translated with fbtee, dates and times
with Temporal, numbers and lists with the browser's own `Intl`, and AI translations, in any
language, including right-to-left ones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is the example app in `apps/example`, deployed to Cloudflare with Void.
- It runs in several languages, including Arabic (right-to-left).
- Sections today: the events app (demo), Docs, Formats and Tour.

## Brand Commitments

- System fonts only. No web fonts.

## Evidence on Hand

- The live site: https://void-fbtee-example.gedw99.workers.dev
- The events app, the Docs and the Tour, in every language the app ships.
- No testimonials, users or benchmarks exist; don't invent any.
